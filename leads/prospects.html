How this was built

  • The profile is based on one past client, Marché Chemin du Roy. I couldn't find its details online or in a connected CRM, so fit is judged on store type and on whether the store already sells online.
  • Found by web search through directories, store sites, Facebook, TikTok and Tripadvisor. No lead-data connector was used. Pages could not be opened directly, so every detail comes from search-result summaries. Check each one by calling.
  • No owner names were found. Every contact is the store's public phone number or page. No email addresses were guessed.
  • Score = fit ×0.4 + signal ×0.4 + reachability ×0.2. Reachability is low for every store because no decision-maker has been named yet.
