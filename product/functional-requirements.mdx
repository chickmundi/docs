<Note>
**Phase 2** of the DealMarket Cameroon build roadmap. Builds directly on the [Product Requirements Document](/product/requirements). Each requirement is tagged with priority — **MVP**, **Wave 2**, or **Wave 3** — matching the delivery waves defined there. Non-functional requirements (performance, security, scalability, availability) are covered separately in Phase 3.
</Note>

## 1. Actors

| Actor | Description |
|---|---|
| **Consumer** | End user of the mobile app; unauthenticated (browsing) or authenticated (personalized features) |
| **Retailer Owner** | Full administrative control over a retailer organization on the web platform |
| **Retailer Staff** | Scoped access to a subset of admin portal functions, per role permissions |
| **Platform Admin** | DealMarket internal staff operating the platform, moderating content, managing retailer onboarding |
| **AI System** | Automated backend processes (OCR, ranking, recommendation, generation) acting on behalf of the platform, always subject to human verification where noted |

Requirement IDs use the pattern `FR-<MODULE>-<NUMBER>`.

## 12. AI OCR Engine

| ID | Requirement | Actor | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-OCR-001 | The system automatically processes uploaded PDF flyers and images to extract per-product: image, name, brand, category, barcode (if present), original price, promotional price, discount percentage, promotion period, store, quantity, and packaging. | AI System | MVP |
| FR-OCR-002 | Each extracted product record includes a confidence score. | AI System | MVP |
| FR-OCR-003 | No AI-extracted promotion is published to consumers until a Retailer Staff user reviews and approves it. | Retailer Staff | MVP |
| FR-OCR-004 | The review interface lets staff correct any extracted field before approval, and flags low-confidence fields for attention. | Retailer Staff | MVP |
| FR-OCR-005 | Rejected or corrected extractions are logged and usable to monitor/improve OCR accuracy over time. | System | Wave 2 |

## 13. Smart Deal Ranking

| ID | Requirement | Actor | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-RANK-001 | The ranking algorithm scores each candidate deal using, at minimum, distance, discount, price, and popularity. | AI System | MVP |
| FR-RANK-002 | The ranking algorithm additionally incorporates user preferences, shopping history, current season, product availability, sponsored priority, store rating, recent searches, and trending signals as they become available. | AI System | Wave 2 |
| FR-RANK-003 | Sponsored placement influences ranking within defined, bounded limits — it cannot fully override relevance for a consumer (e.g., a sponsored deal 50km away does not outrank a highly relevant nearby organic deal). | AI System | MVP |
| FR-RANK-004 | Ranking recomputes as relevant inputs change (new location, new promotion, expired promotion) within an acceptable latency defined in the non-functional requirements. | AI System | MVP |
